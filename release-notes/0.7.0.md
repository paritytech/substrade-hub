Limit orders placed from the app now expire 24 hours after they are sent, unless they fill first. Open
Orders shows when each one expires. An expired order keeps its escrow until a match reaches it, its
price level is purged, or you cancel it; until then it stays listed, marked "Expired", and Cancel
returns the escrow straight away.

Market orders get an "All or nothing" option: the order fills its whole amount in one transaction, or
nothing fills. Limit orders get a "Post only" option: the order only rests on the book, and is rejected
if it would trade on arrival.

Both networks run new contracts:

- A limit order can be **post-only**: it only rests, and reverts with `PostOnlyWouldCross` when its price
  reaches the best price on the other side.
- An order that meets one of the trader's own resting orders cancels that resting order, refunds its
  escrow and keeps matching. Until now the whole transaction reverted with `SelfTrade`.

**For integrators:** `placeLimitOrder` takes a new last argument, `bool postOnly`, so its selector
changes; pass `false` for the old behaviour. The `SelfTrade` error is gone.
