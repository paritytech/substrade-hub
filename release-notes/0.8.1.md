The app moves to polkadot-api v3 and the matching Parity product-sdk releases, which sign transactions
in a host such as dot.li. Trading works as before, and the contracts are unchanged: both networks keep
the 0.8.0 deployment.

  way. Other Substrate wallets sign as before.
- The product-sdk host, signer and transaction packages are on their latest releases.
